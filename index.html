FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to format my sales sheet?

No. Invoicely reads your columns on its own and shows a preview before creating any invoice.

Can I speak in Hindi?

Yes. Speak or type in English, Hindi or Hinglish, whichever feels natural.

Are the invoices GST-ready?

Yes. Your GST number is added and CGST, SGST or IGST is calculated automatically.

Can I bill clients outside India?

Yes. Pro supports USD, EUR and other currencies for international clients.
